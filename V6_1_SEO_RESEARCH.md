# v6.1 SEO Foundation

## Strategic decision
Do not compete as another generic “10 AI tools” list. Current 2026 search results for contractor AI tools are increasingly workflow-specific: estimating, missed calls, scheduling, documentation, follow-up and reviews.

## First priority page
`/best/best-ai-tools-for-contractors`

Why:
- clear commercial research intent;
- multiple real contractor workflows;
- monetization can eventually come from affiliate/partner programs where available;
- the page can internally link to tool reviews and the Finder;
- easier differentiation than a generic “best AI tools” article.

## Editorial guardrails
- No fake hands-on claims.
- No invented time-saved or revenue claims.
- No ranking based on affiliate commission.
- Exact prices are snapshots and must include a checked date.
- AI-generated estimates are described as drafts that require human verification.

## Official-source facts checked Sep 2026
- Jobber: AI Receptionist and plan/add-on information.
- QuoteIQ: AI Estimator, AI credit model and plan pricing.
- Housecall Pro: AI Team / CSR AI and current plan structure.
- CompanyCam: AI reports, summaries, checklists and other documentation features.

## Conversion events now emitted
- `finder_completed`
- `review_clicked`

Events are pushed to `window.dataLayer` and also emitted as `aibiztools:event`.
This keeps the project ready for Google Tag Manager / GA4 without forcing an analytics vendor before launch.
