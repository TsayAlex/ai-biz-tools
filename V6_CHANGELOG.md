# v6 Launch/Data

## Purpose
Move the project from UI iteration toward trustworthy launch data.

## Finder
The task score now dominates the ranking. Business type acts as a secondary signal.
This specifically prevents customer-support products such as Tidio from ranking highly
for a Restaurant + Marketing query simply because the product supports restaurants.

Expected test:
- Restaurants → Marketing → Keep costs low: marketing/content tools should dominate.
- Ecommerce → Customer Support: Tidio should rank strongly.
- Consultants → Productivity: ChatGPT / Claude / Notion AI should rank strongly.
- Agencies → Automation: Zapier should rank strongly.

## Data verification
Pricing notes were refreshed from official vendor sources in September 2026 for:
ChatGPT, Canva, Zapier, Tidio, Buffer, Notion AI, Otter.ai and Jasper.

Pricing is intentionally presented as a checked snapshot, not a permanent promise.
Claude, HubSpot, Descript and Perplexity remain conservative/no-exact-price entries
until their current official pricing is verified to the same standard.
