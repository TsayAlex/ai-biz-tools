# v5 CHANGELOG

## Product goal
Turn the homepage from a passive directory into a decision tool.

## Added
- Interactive 3-step AI Tool Finder.
- Three ranked recommendations.
- Human-readable explanation for every recommendation.
- Reset/start-again flow.
- Mobile styles for the finder.
- Stronger first-screen CTA and positioning.

## Why this matters
A directory asks visitors to do the research themselves. The finder gives them a clear next step and creates a measurable conversion event that can later be tied to review clicks, outbound clicks and affiliate revenue.

## Next recommended build
- Track `finder_started`, `finder_completed`, `review_clicked` and outbound clicks.
- Validate which combinations users select most often.
- Improve rankings from real click/conversion data.
- Create SEO landing pages around the highest-demand business + task combinations.
